A first call is typically 20 minutes. No preparation required on your end — just a brief overview of your business and what you're considering. Everything discussed is confidential.

What's helpful to include

Whether you reach out by email or call first, the following information helps move a first conversation forward efficiently. None of it is required before speaking — share what you're comfortable with.

  • Business name and location — city and state is sufficient.
  • What the business does — services provided, customers served, how long it's been operating.
  • Revenue and profitability range — a rough range is fine. Exact figures are not needed until an NDA is in place.
  • Reason for considering a transition — retirement, health, other plans, or simply ready to move on.
  • Preferred next step — whether you'd like a call, want more information in writing first, or are asking on behalf of a client.
Confidentiality

Every inquiry is handled discreetly.

Old Brookewood Capital does not share information about inbound inquiries with third parties. Employees, customers, and competitors are not contacted at any point without explicit permission. If a conversation does not lead to a transaction, information shared is kept in confidence.
