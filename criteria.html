Criteria


We seek business owners ready to entrust what they've built to a thoughtful next steward.

What we look for

We are focused on enduring, well-run businesses with strong fundamentals, loyal customers, and teams we can support and grow. Below is a clear snapshot of our acquisition criteria.

Financial profile

  • Revenue: $5M to $50M
  • Earnings: $1.5M to $5M (EBITDA or SDE)
  • History: Profitable for 3+ years
  • Margins: Pre-tax profit margin ≥ 10%

Business characteristics

  • Recurring or repeat customer demand
  • Diversified customer base (limited customer concentration)
  • Strong reputation and durable competitive position
  • Opportunities for operational improvement and steady growth
  • Low customer churn and high customer satisfaction

Owner and transition fit

  • Owner seeking a thoughtful transition and long-term steward
  • Willingness to support a defined transition period (flexible timeline)
  • Interest in protecting employees, customers, and legacy
  • Clean, straightforward communication and aligned expectations
Have a business that fits?

If you're considering a transition - even if you're early in the process - we welcome a confidential conversation.
